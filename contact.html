GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shelf life, cold-chain delivery, glass recycling, and juice hygiene.

How long do PureSqueeze cold-pressed juices stay fresh? +
Because our juices are 100% raw, unpasteurized, and free of chemical preservatives, they maintain peak nutritional vitality for 72 hours when kept refrigerated between 2°C and 4°C. We stamp the exact batch harvest and pressing timestamp on every bottle cap.
What are your delivery charges and minimum order values? +
Doorstep delivery is completely FREE on all orders of ₹499 and above across our active Bengaluru delivery zones. For smaller orders below ₹499, a flat cold-chain delivery charge of ₹40 applies.
How does the Glass Bottle Return Reward Program work? +
We are passionate about circular zero-waste living! Simply rinse your empty PureSqueeze glass bottles and return them to our delivery partner on your next order, or drop them off at our Indiranagar store. You will receive an immediate ₹20 discount credit per bottle toward your next purchase.
Can I customize the sweetness or add extra superfood boosters? +
Yes! When you click Customize on our Menu page or order via WhatsApp, you can specify your sweetness level (100% Raw Unsweetened, Raw Forest Honey, or Organic Coconut Sugar) and add vitality boosters like Wild Organic Amla (+₹35), Cold-Pressed Ginger (+₹25), Soaked Chia Seeds (+₹30), or Ceylon Cinnamon (+₹25).
For 3-Day or 5-Day Cleanses, do you deliver all bottles at once? +
Never! To ensure peak botanical enzyme potency, our 3-Day and 5-Day Cleanse programs are delivered in daily fresh batches every morning between 7:00 AM and 8:30 AM. You receive that day's 6 numbered bottles pressed just 2 hours prior to arrival.
How quickly will my order arrive once I confirm on WhatsApp? +
For Indiranagar, Domlur, and Koramangala, our average delivery turnaround is 25 to 35 minutes. For other central Bengaluru pin codes, express dispatch takes 35 to 45 minutes. You will receive immediate WhatsApp order confirmation with live delivery tracking.
