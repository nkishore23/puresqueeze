Our Origin

Why We Refused to Bottle Sugar Water

In 2021, our founder Chef Ananya noticed a troubling reality in Bengaluru supermarkets: bottles labeled "100% Pure" were actually heated to 85°C (destroying live vitamins), blended with pectin, diluted with tap water, and preserved for months on warehouse shelves.

PureSqueeze was born in an Indiranagar test kitchen with a single commercial hydraulic press. Our pledge: we press in small batches every morning at 5:00 AM, bottle in sterilized glass, and deliver chilled within hours.
